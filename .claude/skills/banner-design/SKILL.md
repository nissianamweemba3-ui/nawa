# Banner Design

Multi-format banner creation: social media, ads, website heroes, print.

## Workflow

1. **Gather Requirements** — platform, dimensions, content, brand, style, quantity
2. **Research** — activate `ui-ux-pro-max`, browse Pinterest for references; select 2-3 styles
3. **Design** — HTML/CSS banners + AI-generated visuals (`ai-artist`/`ai-multimodal`)
4. **Export** — Screenshot to PNG at exact dimensions via `chrome-devtools`
5. **Present** — Side-by-side options with rationale; iterate on feedback

## Art Direction Styles (22)

Minimalist, Gradient, Bold Typography, Photo-Based, Illustrated, Geometric, Retro/Vintage, Glassmorphism, 3D/Depth, Neon/Cyberpunk, Duotone, Editorial, Collage, Hand-drawn, Kinetic, Corporate, Luxury, Playful, Abstract, Pattern-based, Split-layout, Dark Cinematic

## Design Rules

- Safe zones: critical content in central 70–80%
- Single CTA per banner, bottom-right, min 44px height
- Max 2 typefaces, min 16px body, ≥32px headline
- Text under 20% for ads (Meta compliance)
- 4.5:1 contrast ratio minimum
- Print: 300 DPI, CMYK, 3–5mm bleed

## Out of Scope

Video editing, full website design, print production — banners only.
